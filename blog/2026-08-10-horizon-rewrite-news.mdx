It’s been a while since our last announcement, but that **doesn’t mean we’ve stopped working**. Quite the opposite, our team is still hard at work on the **Horizon Rewrite** update.
<!-- truncate -->
Over time, more and more ideas keep coming to mind. Every new idea opens the door to another possibility, and before we know it, the entire project becomes bigger and a little more complex.

There’s still a lot we can’t share yet because things are always changing, but today we can announce two new features that are definitely coming!


### **Horizon AI, here to help you!**

The new Horizon will include Horizon AI, powered by our Horizon API.

As it does now, it will bring data from our third-party providers directly into your Discord community. But also, now you will be able to **summarise space-related articles, blogs, images, and other content** without having to leave Discord.

Our goal: bring knowledge directly to your community with just one click.

### **Astronomy Posts**

We’re also introducing **Astronomy Posts!**

You’ll be able to **submit your own astronomy photos** to the Horizon Gallery and view them **directly through a command**.

We’re also looking into ways to **automatically share** these creations with other communities using **Horizon Automations**, always giving full credit to the original creator (future update).

### **Open Source mind for the future..**

And most importantly, **we don’t want Horizon to be a closed ecosystem.**

In the future, **with the consent of our users**, we want to help other developers use Horizon through our API and build their own projects with the data, content, and possibilities that Horizon can create.

We don’t want to create a monopoly. We want to help other developers build, experiment, and create new ways for people to discover and learn about space.
